# Code of Conduct

BayarLab is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and focus discussions on the project's goals.
- Respect privacy. Do not share private communications, credentials, or customer data without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, threats, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberately exposing secrets or real payment/customer data is also out of bounds.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project publicly. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation in response to behavior they judge harmful. They will consider context and aim for a fair, proportionate response.

To report a conduct concern privately, contact the repository maintainers directly through GitHub where available. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers should not disclose the reporter's identity without permission, except when required to address an immediate safety concern.
